Delete your data

Pantry Pal for Android, by Matthew Griffin · Last updated 17 November 2026

Pantry Pal has no sign-up, so there is no account to close. Almost everything you create lives only on your phone, and you can delete all of it yourself in a few seconds.

Step 1: delete everything on your phone

Do either of these:

  • Clear the app's data: Settings → Apps → Pantry Pal → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your inventory, meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, leftovers, prices, receipt scans, settings, and the saved Claude key and account token.
  • Uninstall the app: this removes the same data.

Clearing the data or uninstalling does not remove your phone's record on our service (see below): reinstalling on the same phone reconnects to it, so the plan and allowance carry over.

Step 2: ask us to delete the record on our service

To make the AI features work, our service keeps a small record for each install. It contains no name, email or phone number. If you want that record deleted, email dev@prootsoftware.co.uk with the subject Delete my Pantry Pal data. Because the record is anonymous we may not be able to tell which one is yours, but we will delete it if you can identify it and otherwise do what we reasonably can. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept
