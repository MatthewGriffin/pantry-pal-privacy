Privacy policy

Pantry Pal for Android · Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version: your pantry, meals, shopping lists and receipts are stored on your phone. The AI assistant works by sending what you type, and photos you choose to scan, through our service to Anthropic's Claude. We don't ask for your name or email, we don't show ads and we don't sell data.

Who we are

Pantry Pal is made by Matthew Griffin ("we"). For anything in this policy, contact dev@prootsoftware.co.uk.

What stays on your phone

  • Your inventory, meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, leftovers, prices you record, receipt scans and settings.
  • Receipt photos are read by Claude to find the items and prices (see below). If Claude is not available, they are read on your phone instead.
  • Your backups, if you make them, are files you choose where to save.

We can't see any of this. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from the phone.

What is sent to the AI

When you use the assistant, the app sends Anthropic's Claude (through our service, below) the message you typed, the recent chat, and enough of your pantry, plan and preferences to answer (for example what you have in stock and how many people you cook for). If you scan a packet, a recipe page or a receipt, the photo is sent too. Claude's reply comes back to your phone.

  • Only things you do with the AI are sent. Adding, editing and deleting things by hand never leaves your phone.
  • Anthropic processes this to produce the reply. Their own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Please don't put anything sensitive in the chat that you wouldn't want processed this way.

Our service between the app and Claude

The app talks to a small service we run on Cloudflare. It keeps our Claude access private and counts how much AI each install has used, so that the free and paid allowances work. It passes your messages on to Claude and passes the reply back; it does not store the content of your messages, replies or photos.

What it does store:

  • A random account ID made for your install (not linked to your name, email or phone number).
  • Which plan the account is on, how much of the allowance has been used and when the current period started.
  • If you subscribe: the Google Play purchase token for your subscription and the date your paid plan runs out. This lets us check with Google that the plan is still active and renew or end it without asking you.
  • A scrambled (hashed) form of the network address used to sign up, counted per day to stop people creating huge numbers of accounts. The real address is not stored by us.
  • A scrambled (hashed) form of your phone's Android ID. It lets us give the same account back if you reinstall Pantry Pal on the same phone, so the plan and allowance stay with the phone and cannot be reset by reinstalling. The real ID is not stored by us, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • If your phone is paired with another phone as a household helper (follower): a key that links that phone to the main phone's account, so its AI use counts against the household's allowance and plan. The main phone can switch the key off at any time by removing the device.

Cloudflare, which hosts the service, processes requests as the network operator and may keep ordinary technical logs as described in its own policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

When an account is created, the app asks Google Play to confirm that it is the genuine Pantry Pal installed from Google Play on a real device (Google Play Integrity). Google provides this check and processes it under its own terms. Our service receives only the pass or fail result, uses it to decide whether to make the account, and does not store it. See policies.google.com/privacy.

If you choose to enter your own Claude API key in Settings, the app then talks to Claude directly with your key, and our service is not involved. The key is stored encrypted on your phone.

Food product lookups

When you scan a barcode or look a product up by name, the barcode number or product name is sent to the Open Food Facts database (and its sister databases) to fetch the product details. See openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Syncing between your own devices

If you pair a second phone to share your household's pantry and plans, the two devices talk to each other directly on your own network. That data doesn't pass through our service.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes, packets, recipes and receipts. Photos are used only as described above.
  • Notifications: reminders such as meals planned for today and food to use up.
  • Network access: for the AI, product lookups and syncing between your devices.

Payments

Paid plans are bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card or Google account details. To switch a plan on we receive the purchase token Google Play gives the app and check it with Google, which tells us which plan it is and when it runs out. Google keeps its own records of your purchase under its own policy.

How long we keep things

Content on your phone stays until you delete it. The service keeps the account record described above (including any purchase token) for as long as the account exists, and the sign-up counters for a short time. Messages and photos are not kept by us.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete everything on your phone by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Clearing the data or uninstalling the app does not remove the account record on our service: reinstalling on the same phone reconnects to it (this is what stops the free allowance being reset). To have the record removed from our service, email us. It holds no name or email, so we may need the account ID, which we can't look up for you, but we will do what we reasonably can.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

Pantry Pal is not aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

The connection between the app and our service is encrypted. Your Claude key, if you add one, and your account token are stored encrypted on your phone. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and updated.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will say so in the app.
